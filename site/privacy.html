Privacy notice

Station Access Guide is part of Rail Club, an unofficial, volunteer-run site for Indian rail travellers. It is not run by Indian Railways or IRCTC. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

Just reading the site

Searching and reading station pages needs no account. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't record which stations you look at.

When you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you press "Sign in with Google", we receive your Google account's name, email address, profile picture and an account ID. We use them to show your name and picture to you in the menu, and to list your own contributions on your profile page. Nobody else sees them. If you're signed in, your browser also remembers the last few stations you opened so it can show them to you; that list stays on your device and is never sent to us.

When you send a visitor report

If you choose to record a video of a station's facilities, we collect:

  • Your Google account's name, email address and an account ID, through "Sign in with Google", to stop spam and to keep track of how reliable each contributor's reports are. We never see your Google password.
  • Your location at the moment you record, only to check you are at the station.
  • The video (no sound is recorded), and the facilities you said it shows.

What happens to your video

  • It is stored privately, never published, and can't be seen by other visitors.
  • It is checked automatically, including by Google's Gemini AI service, and may be watched by a Rail Club reviewer.
  • It is deleted as soon as it has been checked. A video waiting for a reviewer is deleted after 14 days at most, even if no one reviews it.
  • Only the facts are published (for example "Lift: seen by a visitor on 22 Oct 2026"), never your name, email, location or the video itself.

How long we keep the rest

We keep a record of each report (station, facilities, date, the automatic check result and your account ID) so published facts can be traced and spam can be stopped. You can ask us to delete your account and these records at any time; published facts stay on the site without any link to you.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase stores accounts, reports and the temporary videos (servers in Mumbai, India).
  • Google provides sign-in and the Gemini service that checks videos. Rail Club uses Gemini's paid service, under which Google does not use the videos to improve its products.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.

Please don't film people

Film only the facility, not other passengers. Every video is deleted after it has been checked, and only the facilities are published, so people who happen to be in a video are never shown on the site.

Your rights and contact

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent. Write to railclub.support@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 days.
